Roster: the IOC's 206 National Olympic Committees (per direct request) -- the 193 UN member states
plus the 13 additional entities the IOC recognizes separately (Palestine, Cook Islands, Kosovo,
Taiwan, Hong Kong, Puerto Rico, American Samoa, Aruba, Bermuda, British Virgin Islands, Cayman
Islands, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands) -- plus the Pride flag (pride.svg, drawn for this project,
six-stripe rainbow) = 207 flags total. Holy See/Vatican was dropped from an earlier UN-based roster
since it has no NOC and doesn't compete at the Olympics.
