Roster: full ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 currently-assigned list (249 codes) + Kosovo (xk) = 250 flags and
territories, minus 13 removed as visual duplicates of another entry already in the roster (a
dependent territory using its sovereign country's flag unaltered -- e.g. French Guiana/Guadeloupe/
etc. all fly the plain French tricolor already represented by France itself), plus the Pride flag
(pride.svg, drawn for this project, six-stripe rainbow) = 238 flags total.

Duplicates removed (kept the sovereign country's entry in each case): Bonaire/Sint Eustatius/Saba,
French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Heard Island and McDonald Islands, Mayotte, Réunion, Saint Barthélemy,
Saint Helena/Ascension/Tristan da Cunha, Saint Martin, Saint Pierre and Miquelon, Svalbard and
Jan Mayen, United States Minor Outlying Islands, Wallis and Futuna.
